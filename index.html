Professional Background

Three roles, one regional focus

Kroum Vassilev's current work is organized around three concurrent roles: Managing Director of Profine Energy Bulgaria, a joint venture between Profine and Wirth Gruppe focused on large-scale renewable energy projects; Principal at Enevlo, where he is involved in the planning, financing, construction, and operation of power plants and energy storage systems; and Operating Partner at Nebula Purple, a venture capital firm supporting early-stage companies across the Southeast European region.

Each role sits within Bulgaria's growing renewable energy and technology sectors, and together they reflect a cross-sector approach spanning infrastructure development and early-stage investment.
