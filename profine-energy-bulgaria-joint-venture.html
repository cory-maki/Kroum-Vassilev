Profine Energy Bulgaria: Building Renewable Capacity Through Joint Venture

📅 August 2026
⌚ 4 min read

Profine Energy Bulgaria, a joint venture between Profine and Wirth Gruppe, is developing large-scale renewable energy projects in Bulgaria under the leadership of Managing Director Kroum Vassilev.

Profine Energy Bulgaria was formed as a joint venture between Profine and Wirth Gruppe to pursue large-scale renewable energy development in Bulgaria. Kroum Vassilev serves as Managing Director of the venture, overseeing its work within the country's growing clean energy sector.

A Joint Venture Structure

Joint ventures are a common structure for renewable energy development because they combine the technical and financial resources of two organizations. In this case, Profine and Wirth Gruppe bring together their respective capabilities to pursue projects at a scale that would be harder for either partner to take on alone.

This structure allows Profine Energy Bulgaria to address the growing demand for clean energy infrastructure across Bulgaria and the broader Southeast European region.

Bulgaria's Renewable Energy Context

Bulgaria has seen increased interest from European energy investors as part of the continent's broader shift toward renewable generation. Building renewable energy and related infrastructure in Bulgaria carries strategic relevance within that wider European clean energy transition.

Vassilev's work at Profine Energy Bulgaria connects to his other principal role at Enevlo, where his involvement extends into power plant and energy storage system development more broadly. Further detail on that work is available in the profile of Kroum Vassilev's cross-sector background.

Conclusion

Profine Energy Bulgaria represents one part of a larger set of renewable energy commitments in Bulgaria led in part by Kroum Vassilev, alongside his venture capital work at Nebula Purple.
