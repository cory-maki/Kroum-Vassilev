Kroum Vassilev: Entrepreneur at the Intersection of Energy and Technology

📅 August 2026
⌚ 4 min read

Kroum Vassilev holds three concurrent operating roles spanning renewable energy infrastructure and early-stage venture capital, with a consistent focus on Bulgaria and the wider Southeast European region.

Kroum Vassilev's professional background spans technology, renewable energy, and real estate. His current portfolio of roles reflects that range: Managing Director of Profine Energy Bulgaria, Principal at Enevlo, and Operating Partner at Nebula Purple, each active in Bulgaria or the broader Southeast European market.

Renewable Energy and Infrastructure

At Profine Energy Bulgaria, Vassilev leads a joint venture between Profine and Wirth Gruppe developing large-scale renewable energy projects. Through Enevlo, his involvement extends across the full project lifecycle of power infrastructure — planning, financing, construction, and operation of power plants and energy storage systems.

Bulgaria has drawn increased interest from European energy investors in recent years, and infrastructure work of this kind sits at the center of that shift toward cleaner, more distributed power generation.

Three roles across two sectors: renewable energy infrastructure through Profine Energy Bulgaria and Enevlo, and early-stage investment through Nebula Purple.

Venture Capital in the SEE Region

Vassilev's third role, as Operating Partner at Nebula Purple, brings a different kind of work: supporting early-stage companies in deep tech, green energy, fintech, and artificial intelligence across the Southeast European region. That work includes fundraising support, strategic input, and scaling operations for portfolio companies.

The SEE venture ecosystem remains earlier-stage than Western Europe's, which shapes how firms like Nebula Purple approach sourcing and supporting deals in the region.

Operating Across Sectors

Holding leadership positions in both long-cycle infrastructure projects and faster-moving early-stage investment requires working across different capital structures, regulatory environments, and timelines. Vassilev's cross-sector background in technology, energy, and real estate informs how he approaches both sides of that work.

More detail on each individual role is available in the profile pieces on Profine Energy Bulgaria's joint venture and on Nebula Purple's venture capital focus.

Conclusion

Kroum Vassilev's current work is best understood as a set of parallel commitments across renewable energy infrastructure and venture capital, unified by a consistent geographic focus on Bulgaria and Southeast Europe.
